# FAQ

**Question:** The message “Please select a starting point from the menu
in the settings” appears.

Answer: No starting point has been selected—or the entered value is
neither a menu ID nor an address containing one. In the
**Select Start Point** window, click a menu item on the left, click **Apply**, and
save. This message also appears on the published page, so never
publish without a start point.

**Question:** The message “The menu could not be loaded” appears.

Answer: The widget cannot access the menu item. Common causes: the
menu item was deleted, the reader is not authorized to view it, or the
connection was briefly lost. **Try Again** will attempt to access it once more; if the
message persists, select a new starting point.

**Question:** Only the starting point appears in the bar.

Answer: The starting point has no visible subitems—either none at all,
or all of them are set to “Not visible” in the menu or are blocked for the
reader.

**Question:** A drop-down menu says, “There are no further entries here.”

Answer: The entry does have subitems, but none of them are visible to the
reader or lead to a page.

**Question:** Why doesn’t an entry have an arrow?

Answer: The arrow only appears if the entry in the menu has subitems
that the reader is allowed to see and that are not set to “Hidden.”
Clicking on the title then simply opens the page. The root item has
no arrow if it is at the very top of the menu or if there are no
visible menu items next to it.

**Question:** An entry is missing from the navigation, even though it’s in the menu.

Answer: If it is set to “Hidden” in the menu, it will not appear in the
navigation either. Additionally, each user only sees the menu items they
are authorized to view—as an editor, you may see more than your
readers.

**Question:** Not all subitems fit in the bar.

Answer: In that case, the **‹ ›** buttons appear on the right; they scroll the bar
further. On a phone, you can also swipe sideways with your finger
to scroll it. At the beginning or end, the respective button is grayed out.

**Question:** Can I rename or reorder entries just for this widget?

Answer: No. Titles and order come from the app’s menu; changes
made there apply everywhere, including in the navigation.

**Question:** A menu item can’t be clicked in the window.

Answer: Only menu items with subitems can be selected—only they fill a
bar. Pages without subitems (subline “Page”) are listed in the tree
for orientation.

**Question:** A menu item is missing from the window’s tree.

Answer: The tree loads the menu up to a fixed depth and size, and it
shows only what your account is authorized to see. Enter the menu item via
**Enter ID or Address** in the lower-left corner: the address of its page
or the 24-digit ID.
